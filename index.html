My approach

My practice is cultivated within a holistic framework, acknowledging that mental health is an ecosystem of thoughts, emotions, and actions. I ground this work in Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs, ensuring we address foundational well-being to support sustainable, long-term happiness rather than temporary fixes. I approach each client’s situation with the mindset of adapting my methodology to the individual rather than forcing the individual into a modality.

This flexibility is set in the tenets of Alfred Adler’s Individual Psychology, specifically the belief that true well-being is rooted in an individual’s capacity to collaborate, contribute, and connect with the world around them. My goal is to help nurture that inherent ability. By maintaining a person-centered focus, I attune to the strengths and habits already presented, ensuring that our work together is not a prescription, but a personalized pathway to meaningful change.
